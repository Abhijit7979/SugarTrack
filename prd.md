# SugarTrack – website

## 🚀 Elevator Pitch

**SugarTrack** is a gamified website designed to boost your learning discipline and reward your research efforts. Whether you're a student, researcher, or tech enthusiast, SugarTrack monitors your time spent on high-value websites, quizzes you on what you learned, and lets you earn Sugar Coins you can redeem for real-world perks like hackathon passes, merch, and mentorships.

---

## 👥 Who is this App For

- **Students and Researchers** tracking time and knowledge.
- **Professionals & Lifelong Learners** improving productivity.
- **Hobbyists & Tech Enthusiasts** exploring new topics.
- Anyone who researches online and wants to stay motivated.

---

## 🔧 Functional Requirements

### 🎯 Research Tracking & Coin Rewards

- Tracks time spent on high-value websites.
- Users set a **custom daily research goal**.
- Earn +1 Sugar Coin every 30 mins of research.
- Daily login bonus: +1 Sugar Coin.
- Penalties:
  - -3 Coins for inactivity (no research that day).
  - -3 Coins if under 30% of daily goal.
- Unlimited earning potential.

### 🧠 Daily Summary + AI Quiz

- Users write a short summary of what they learned.
- AI generates 3–5 multiple-choice quiz questions.
- Earn Sugar Coins for each correct answer.

### 💬 Sticky Notifications

- Creative, clingy notifications:
  - Motivational quotes
  - Research streak reminders
  - Fun facts and nudges

### 🤖 Agentic AI Chatbot

- Built-in AI chatbot with **real-time web search**.
- Can answer queries, summarize pages, assist with research.

### 🏆 Ranking System

- Global leaderboard by Sugar Coin count.
- Weekly and monthly top users.

### 👤 User Profile

- Username and email
- Custom daily goal setting
- Graphical timeline of time spent on research
- Coin history + progress chart

### 🎁 Rewards Marketplace (In-App)

- Redeem Sugar Coins for:
  - Hackathon & networking event passes
  - Hoodies, caps, and swag
  - Mentorship sessions
- Inventory and availability displayed
- Instant coin deduction and confirmation

## 🛠 Tech Overview

- **Platform**: website
- **Authentication**: Email/password
- **AI Tools**: LLM for summary parsing and quiz generation
- **Agentic Chatbot**: Web-search capable via browser integration
- **Frontend**: Flask
- **Backend (Optional)**: Firebase, Supabase, or any lightweight serverless backend for auth, coin tracking, and reward management
